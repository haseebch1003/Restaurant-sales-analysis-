# Project Summary and Key Insights

This document summarizes the key findings from the pizza sales analysis project. All results are based on validated SQL queries executed in Azure Data Studio and cross checked with Power BI visuals.

## Key Performance Indicators

- Total Revenue: $817,860.05  
- Total Orders: 21,350  
- Total Pizzas Sold: 49,574  
- Average Order Value: $38.31  
- Average Pizzas Per Order: 2.32  

These KPIs provide a high level view of overall business performance and customer purchasing behavior.

## Ordering Trends

### Orders by Day of Week

Order volume varied across the week, with the highest number of orders occurring on Fridays and Saturdays.

- Friday recorded the highest number of orders with 3,538 orders  
- Saturday followed closely with 3,158 orders  
- Sunday had the lowest weekend order volume with 2,624 orders  

This pattern suggests that customer demand peaks toward the end of the work week and remains strong through the weekend.

### Orders by Month

Monthly analysis showed noticeable seasonality in ordering behavior.

- July recorded the highest number of orders with 1,935 orders  
- May and January also showed strong performance with 1,853 and 1,845 orders respectively  
- October and September recorded the lowest order volumes  

These trends indicate higher customer demand during summer months, with a gradual decline toward the end of the year.

## Product and Category Performance

### Sales Contribution by Pizza Category

Sales were relatively evenly distributed across categories, with a slight lead from the Classic category.

- Classic pizzas accounted for 26.91 percent of total sales  
- Supreme pizzas contributed 25.46 percent  
- Chicken pizzas contributed 23.96 percent  
- Veggie pizzas contributed 23.68 percent  

This balance suggests that maintaining variety across categories is important for sustaining revenue.

### Top 5 Pizzas by Revenue

A small number of pizzas generated a significant portion of total revenue.

The top performing pizzas by revenue were:
- The Thai Chicken Pizza with $43,434.25 in revenue  
- The Barbecue Chicken Pizza with $42,768.00 in revenue  
- The California Chicken Pizza with $41,409.50 in revenue  
- The Classic Deluxe Pizza with $38,180.50 in revenue  
- The Spicy Italian Pizza with $34,831.25 in revenue  

These pizzas represent key products that drive consistent sales and could be prioritized in promotions and menu placement.

## Business Insights

- End of week demand peaks suggest opportunities for targeted promotions and staffing optimization on Fridays and Saturdays  
- Seasonal trends indicate increased demand during summer months, which could inform inventory and marketing planning  
- Revenue is evenly distributed across pizza categories, reducing reliance on a single product type  
- A small set of pizzas consistently drives top line revenue, making them strong candidates for featured offerings  

## Conclusion

This analysis provides a clear view of overall business performance, customer ordering patterns, and product level contribution. The combination of SQL based validation and Power BI visualization ensures accuracy while delivering  insights that can support business decision making.
